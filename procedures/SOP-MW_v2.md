## Purpose
Defines how dispatch responds when a delivery is not completed inside the promised window.

## Immediate checks
Confirm the latest scan and driver location from the route system. If the package has no scan in the last three hours, also follow SOP-MS.

## Customer notification
Notify the customer within 30 minutes after the window closes. State only confirmed facts. Do not promise a new arrival time unless the route system provides one.

## Rescheduling
If the driver cannot reach the stop within 60 minutes of the window end, offer the next available window and record the customer's choice in a dispatch note.

## Ownership
The regional dispatch lead owns the response. Escalate to the regional operations manager if the shipment is more than two hours past the window.
