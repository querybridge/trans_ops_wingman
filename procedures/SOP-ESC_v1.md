## Roles
The regional dispatch lead owns day-of-delivery exceptions in their region. Customer service owns customer rescheduling and disputes after handoff. The regional operations manager owns escalations and cross-team decisions.

## When to escalate
Escalate to the regional operations manager when a shipment is more than two hours past its window, when a package cannot be located within two hours, or when driver and customer accounts conflict.

## Recording decisions
Every escalation decision is recorded in a dispatch note with the owner's role and the time.
