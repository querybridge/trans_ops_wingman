## Requirement
Every delivered shipment needs a proof of delivery: a photo of the package at the delivery location, or a recipient signature.

## Grace period
The driver must upload proof of delivery within 60 minutes of marking the shipment delivered.

## Missing proof
If proof is missing after the grace period, contact the driver to upload it from the handheld. If it cannot be recovered, ask the customer to confirm receipt and record the confirmation in a dispatch note.

## Ownership
The regional dispatch lead owns POD recovery. Do not close the delivery as complete until proof or customer confirmation is recorded.
