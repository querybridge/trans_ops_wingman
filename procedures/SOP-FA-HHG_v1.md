## Scope
Applies only to Harborview Home Goods shipments. Other customers follow their own failed-attempt procedures.

## Same-day reattempt
If the delivery window is still open, schedule one same-day reattempt on the current route. Confirm the address and access details with the customer before the reattempt.

## Disputed attempts
If the customer disputes that an attempt occurred, request the driver's handheld location log and door-tag photo before reattempting. Record both accounts in dispatch notes and mark the case for review.

## Second failure
After a second failed attempt, return the package to the hub and hand the case to customer service to arrange a new window.

## Ownership
The Tampa, Orlando, or Jacksonville dispatch lead owns reattempts. Customer service owns rescheduling after a second failure.
