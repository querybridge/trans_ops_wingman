## Thresholds
A package in transit or picked up must be scanned at least every six hours. A package out for delivery must be scanned at least every three hours.

## Locating the package
Ask the hub or cross-dock lead to physically locate the package and scan it. Check the last known trailer or route assignment.

## Customer communication
Do not tell the customer the package is on its way until a new scan confirms its location.

## Ownership
The regional dispatch lead owns the search. Escalate to the regional operations manager if the package is not located within two hours.
