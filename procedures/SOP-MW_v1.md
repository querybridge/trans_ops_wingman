## Purpose
Defines how dispatch responds when a delivery will not be completed inside the promised window. Superseded by version v2 on 2026-07-01.

## Customer notification
Notify the customer within 60 minutes after the window closes and offer the next available window.

## Ownership
The regional dispatch lead owns the response until the shipment is delivered or rescheduled.
